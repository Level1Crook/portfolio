## ✅ Design & UX

- [ ] **Visual Hierarchy**
  - [ ] Most important information stands out
  - [ ] Sections are clearly separated
  - [ ] Headings are properly sized
  - [ ] Whitespace is used effectively

- [ ] **Dark Mode**
  - [ ] Toggle button is visible

- [ ] **Responsiveness**
  - [ ] Mobile view is clean and readable

**Design & UX** (1-10): \_\_\_

- Is the design professional and easy to navigate?

**Trust Building** (1-10): \_\_\_

- Does your site build confidence in hiring you?

**Call-to-Action Strength** (1-10): \_\_\_

- Are CTAs clear, visible, and compelling?

**Technical Excellence** (1-10): \_\_\_

- Is the site fast, bug-free, and accessible?

**Overall Conversion Readiness** (1-10): \_\_\_

---

**Target Score: 85+ before deployment**

If scoring below 85:

1. Identify lowest-scoring areas
2. Improve content or design
3. Test with someone else
4. Re-score and deploy when ready
